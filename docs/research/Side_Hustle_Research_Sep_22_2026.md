Side Hustle Research
Research date: September 22, 2026
Timing: This research happened during NAT-TEST N4 exam week. The plan is to start executing after the exam on September 27, 2026.
Quick take
* Best near-term fit: Paid research studies and small freelance dev/data gigs. They match an AI/data profile and can be tested without building an audience first.
* Good experiments: Resume writing, UGC work, user testing, and a small no-code or AI tool.
* Slow-burn option: YouTube Shorts can build reach, but ad revenue takes substantial scale and Indian-audience RPMs tend to sit at the low end.
* Low relevance from India: US class-action settlement finder apps.

Paid research studies
User Interviews supports participants in India. The flow is to join the participant panel, apply to individual studies, complete a screener, and wait for acceptance. Typical studies may pay about $20-$100 or more, depending on the format and target profile.

Caveats: The listed deadlines were in early October. Pay figures came from a third-party mirror rather than the official listings, and each screener determines whether the applicant is accepted.
Settlement finder apps
Apps and sites such as Settlemate, Collect / settlementfinder.com, ClaimBee, SettleScout, and Payout help people find class-action lawsuit settlements they may qualify for and file claims.
Fit: These services are mainly aimed at US consumers and are likely to be mostly useless from India.
Other lanes discussed
* Freelance dev/data gigs: Upwork, Fiverr, and Contra. A narrow offer is easier to sell than a general “I can code” profile, such as dashboard cleanup, Python automation, data analysis, or small AI integrations.
* UGC video work: Create videos for brands on a per-video basis. Followers are not required because brands pay for the content itself, not access to an existing audience.
* Micro1-style AI data work: A previous application quoted $31/hour for 20 hours per week. The next-stage link lapses on September 24, 2026 because exam prep takes priority. Revisit similar work after N4.
After-exam starting plan

2. Choose one narrow freelance dev/data offer and prepare one proof sample.
3. Pick one lightweight experiment from resume writing, UGC, or a small no-code AI tool.
4. Treat YouTube as a longer-term audience project, not immediate income.
